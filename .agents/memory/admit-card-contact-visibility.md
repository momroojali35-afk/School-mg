---
name: Admit-card contact visibility
description: Product decision for school contact details on generated admit cards.
---

The school phone number and email address should not appear in admit-card headers or the admit-card preview; they remain available to other school-management features.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested removing those details without changing any other functions or UI.

**How to apply:** Scope future contact-detail changes to the admit-card templates and preview unless the user explicitly asks to change shared school branding.
