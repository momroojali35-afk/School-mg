---
name: Attendance percentage semantics
description: How attendance reports treat inactive student days.
---

Attendance percentage uses present divided by present + absent + inactive; holidays are excluded, while inactive is shown as its own count.

**Why:** Inactive students are no longer part of normal teacher marking, but their missed school days must still reduce the student's attendance percentage.

**How to apply:** Preserve the inactive status when adding or displaying attendance records in both admin and teacher views.
